03

Undergraduate Thesis

Undergraduate thesis — Bachelor of Architecture and Urbanism · UTFPR · 2025

Territories at Risk, Networked Solutions

Urban Resilience Plan for the Enxofre Stream Watershed in Piracicaba, Brazil

Honourable Mention — Rosa Kliass Award

Honourable Mention, 10th Rosa Kliass Award — National University Competition in Landscape Architecture (Brazilian Association of Landscape Architects, ABAP), 2026 results, Landscape Planning category, Region 05 — South.

Official results (ABAP) (Opens in a new tab)

The thesis starts from the historical rupture between rivers and urbanisation to propose an urban resilience plan for the middle course of the Enxofre stream in Piracicaba — the area with the most informal settlements in the municipality. The diagnosis combines physical-environmental, socioeconomic, legal and community data; the proposal organises green infrastructure, linear parks, social housing, new centralities and revised zoning into three integrated axes.

Location

Middle course of the Enxofre stream watershed, in the western and southern parts of Piracicaba's urban area: a landscape unit covering eight neighbourhoods and 5.5 km².

Problem

The area has the largest number of mapped informal settlements and 43% of Piracicaba's low-income population. Steep terrain, low permeability, degraded riparian vegetation and occupied valley floors lead to flooding, landslide-prone areas and heat islands, while leisure, culture and mobility facilities are concentrated in the city centre.

Objective

Develop an urban resilience plan for the valley floor of the Enxofre stream's middle course — considered the most socially vulnerable part of Piracicaba's urban area — fostering new ways of using public space and the social function of the city.

Proposal overview

  • Connectivity: revised street system with green infrastructure and readjusted street cross-sections, revised zoning with restructured special zones and new protection, recovery and environmental interest zones.
  • Urban Life: identification of potential for two new sub-centres with public facilities, ReUrb (land regularisation) for social-interest settlements and resettlement for 907 families in risk areas.
  • Ecosystem: revitalisation of the existing linear parks with wider walkways and permeable banks, and a proposed Enxofre Linear Park along 5.24 km of watercourses with bioengineering.

04

About

I am an architect and urban planner trained at the Federal University of Technology – Paraná (UTFPR), and I work with territorial data: I organise public datasets, structure spatial databases, produce indicators and maps, and turn results into assessments, dashboards and critical technical reports for the people who plan and make decisions about cities.

Between 2023 and 2025, I was part of technical teams on metropolitan and municipal plans — the Integrated Urban Development Plan for the Curitiba Metropolitan Region (PDUI-RMC) and the João Pessoa Master Plan — and, at IPPUC, Curitiba's urban planning institute, I supported technical studies for the revision of Curitiba's Master Plan.

In my independent projects I combine PostGIS, Python and QGIS to answer concrete questions about access to services, exposure to environmental hazards and public transport supply, always stating methods, sources and limitations.
