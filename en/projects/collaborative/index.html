02 IPPUC · Power BI

Population and Households in Curitiba — First Results — 2022 Census

Interactive Power BI dashboard (public web report)

Institution
IPPUC — Curitiba Institute for Urban Planning and Research, Monitoring and Research Coordination
Period
Sep 2024 – Dec 2025
Role
Internship at IPPUC's Monitoring and Research Coordination
Location
Curitiba, Paraná, Brazil

Context

IPPUC public dashboard presenting the first 2022 Census results for Curitiba, built from IBGE census-tract aggregates and the CNEFE address register.

Scope

Two-page dashboard showing resident population, private and collective dwellings, occupied and unoccupied dwellings, other establishment types, population density and 2010–2022 change by neighbourhood, administrative region and census tract, with a 1980–2022 time series.

  • Power BI
  • Censo 2022 (IBGE)
  • CNEFE

My contribution — activities

  • Prepared and reviewed the data.
  • Checked the consistency of the information.
  • Modelled the visualisation.
  • Handled the final publication of the dashboard.

Institutional IPPUC dashboard developed by its Monitoring and Research Coordination. It is not an individually authored work.

03 URBTEC · AMEP

PDUI-RMC — Integrated Urban Development Plan for the Curitiba Metropolitan Region

Metropolitan plan (statutory instrument under Brazil's Metropolis Statute)

Institution
Coordinated by AMEP — Paraná Metropolitan Affairs Agency, with the PDUI-RMC Sustentável Consortium.
Period
Feb 2023 – Jul 2024
Role
Internship on the URBTEC technical team
Location
Curitiba Metropolitan Region, Paraná, Brazil

Context

The PDUI is the metropolitan planning instrument required by Brazil’s Metropolis Statute. In the Curitiba Metropolitan Region, its preparation is coordinated by AMEP.

Scope

Involvement linked to phases 1 (mobilisation and work plan), 2 (priority sector diagnostics) and 5 (processes for priority functions of common interest and the inter-federative governance model).

My contribution — activities

  • Collected and analysed socioeconomic and physical-territorial information.
  • Organised and systematised territorial data.
  • Prepared and organised charts, maps and tables.
  • Carried out technical editing of report volumes.
  • Supported the monitoring and critical assessment of public policies.

A collectively produced plan coordinated by AMEP. The participation described refers to support activities within the technical team in phases 1, 2 and 5.
